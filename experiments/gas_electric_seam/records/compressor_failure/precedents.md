# Compressor-station failure precedents (grounding rule)

Each failure mode in the compressor-failure scenario set cites at least one real event.

| Mode | Event | What happened | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| isolate | Kermit Compressor Station, Northern Natural Gas, Winkler County TX, June 2003 | Explosions and fire; station gas supply cut off; force majeure, no deliveries through the station | NGI; Power Engineering (5 Jun 2003) |
| isolate | Prairie Lea Compressor Station, Oasis Pipeline (Waha-to-Katy, Permian to southeast Texas), Caldwell County TX | 36-inch line ruptured near the station; station shut down under force majeure | NGI; Railroad Commission of Texas |
| isolate | Owingsville Compressor Station, Texas Eastern Line 10, KY, 4 May 2020 | Rupture and explosion north of the station; throughput through the area set to zero | Wood Mackenzie |
| bypass | Centerville station, Tennessee Gas Pipeline, Hickman County TN, Aug 2023 | Equipment failure caused explosion and fire; station shut until about 1 Oct 2023 while the pipeline stayed in service | Pipeline Journal; Reuters via P&GJ |
| context | Winter Storm Uri, Feb 2021 | FERC-NERC final report: loss of electricity to natural gas facilities was a predominant factor in reduced gas supply | FERC-NERC staff report (Nov 2021) |

Testbed station chosen for the Houston test: Victoria-area station (GasTranSim compressors 9 and 24, ratio 1.1 each),
which sits on the south-coast supply path into the Houston plants, the same corridor role as Prairie Lea on Waha-to-Katy.
